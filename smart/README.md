## 🏆 Signature Achievement · Kafr El-Sheikh 2024

> **Situation:** Smart Digital Services took over a Ministry of Supply subsidy project from a competing company. The Ministry gave a strict **24-hour window** to migrate and register all merchant data before going live.

> **Challenge:** 4,000 merchants. 14 data fields each. Sensitive bank account details for profit transfers. Only **5 people available** including myself.

> **What I did:** While three team members entered data manually and one validated, I identified that the manual approach would not meet the deadline. I spent **5 hours building a Python automation script** that extracted structured data from the PDF source files and injected it directly into the company system.

> **Result:** By the time the script was ready, the manual team had completed 20% of records. The script completed the remaining 80% in approximately 2 hours. A structured verification pass confirmed data integrity.

✅ **Project went live on schedule. Zero data errors. Zero deadline breach.**
